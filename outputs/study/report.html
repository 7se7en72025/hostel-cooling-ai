Preference-aware cooling for student hostels

Download the full submission PDF

Completed computational scope

Actual Qwen3-4B transformer inference, persistent activity preferences, trained neural and routine models, noisy-data calibration, complete course baseline sets and reproducible evidence. Room and occupant outcomes remain explicitly simulated.

Research design

The study runs 7 days for each of 3 weather seeds. Room physics advances every 15 minutes. All 9 controllers choose actions on the same 60-minute cadence. Each policy evolves its own closed-loop room state under common weather and constraints.

Actual local language inference

Model: Qwen3-4B-Q4_K_M / llama.cpp b11756. Structured extractions require original-message evidence quotes and validated values. Study prompts, responses, usage and timings are preserved. Matching requests can reuse labeled cached records. Recorded-response replay cannot generate a new answer.

Held-out language benchmark: 24 phrases, 70.8% exact-match accuracy and field F1 0.727. All successes, misses and rejected invalid preferences remain available.

The benchmark measures raw extraction without human correction. The application shows proposed preference changes for explicit confirmation or discard before saving. Preview leaves memory unchanged, and confirmation rechecks consent and the profile revision.

Calibrated room model

Fourteen days of noisy synthetic observations fit the controller model. Training and test transitions are chronological and separate. Held-out RMSE: 0.180 C, versus 0.233 C for persistence. The fitted conductance differs from the known plant value, illustrating parameter identifiability and noise limitations.

What the findings establish

The results establish functioning software, actual local model behavior and an auditable simulated comparison. Comfort and acceptance derive from a synthetic occupant oracle related to the modeling assumptions. They do not establish measured savings or a human preference-learning benefit. Paired t intervals across three weather seeds are exploratory scenario summaries.

EPI covers fan kWh per floor area during the simulated period. It excludes other building energy and makes no annual or certification claim. Physical actuators, measured field outcomes and multi-room human deployment are outside the approved simulated scope.
